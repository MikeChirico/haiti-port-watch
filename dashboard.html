What a count isVessels present in a port's count area when Sentinel-2 passes, about 10:25 local time, on clear days only. A ship that stays a week is counted on every clear pass.
Monthly figuresThe mean over that month's clear days. Months with fewer than 2 clear days are flagged “low”. Never sum daily rows into a monthly total.
AccuracyAgainst a human review of 56 passes, counts total 0.92× the reviewer's (r = 0.95). Port-au-Prince reads about 25% low, Cap-Haïtien about 25% high, Port Lafito about 50% high.
How to cite and reuse

Haiti Port Watch (2026). Vessel counts at Port-au-Prince, Cap-Haïtien and Port Lafito from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, October 2021 – October 2026. Export of 22 October 2026. https://mikechirico.github.io/haiti-port-watch/

When you reuse the data, credit Haiti Port Watch and include “Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2021–2026”. Counts are snapshots on clear days; read the notes on this page before comparing them with other sources.
